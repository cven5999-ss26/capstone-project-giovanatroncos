## Methodological information

1.  Description of methods used for collection/generation of data:
Data were collected between 2012 and 2022 by EWB-USA chapters involved in project construction, 
in collaboration with partner NGOs and participating communities. Records were compiled into a 
structured spreadsheet covering projects in Peru.

2.  Methods for processing the data:
Students compiled and processed the data under the supervision of senior engineers.
